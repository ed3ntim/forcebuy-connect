# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **contact@forcebuy.net**, in English or German. No PGP key; please do not
send working secrets (session cookies, tokens), describe how to reach them
instead. Include what you found and where, steps to reproduce, and the impact.

- Acknowledgement within 7 days.
- Our assessment within 30 days.
- Please give us time to fix the issue before you publish details. We name you
  in the fix notes if you wish.

## Scope

In scope: forcebuy.net, its API, the ForceBuy Connect browser extension and the
local app. Not in scope: Steam, the marketplaces ForceBuy reads from, and
Cloudflare itself.

## Rules for testing

- Use only your own accounts and data; do not access anyone else's.
- No denial of service, no automated mass scanning.
- Do not act against Steam or a marketplace in ForceBuy's name.

There is no bug bounty.

Full text: https://forcebuy.net/security. Machine-readable:
https://forcebuy.net/.well-known/security.txt.
